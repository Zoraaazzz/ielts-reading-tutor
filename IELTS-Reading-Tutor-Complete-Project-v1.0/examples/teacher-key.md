# Borrow, Learn, Repair: A Library Experiment

**Original practice / 原创练习** · Suggested level: 5.5 · Time: 18 minutes

*Original fictional educational passage; not an official IELTS paper*

## Reading passage

**A.** When Riverton Library launched its Object Lending Corner in 2021, staff expected sewing machines to be popular. Instead, the most frequently borrowed items in the first month were portable projectors. The collection later expanded to include board games, sewing machines and digital thermometers. Each object could be borrowed for seven days with a library card, and no borrowing fee was charged. The project grew from conversations with residents who wanted equipment for occasional tasks but hesitated to buy products they might use only once or twice. The library did not advertise the project as a substitute for local shops.

**B.** At first, librarians worried about objects being damaged or returned late. There were 18 reports of minor damage during the first quarter. Staff then introduced ten-minute demonstrations and illustrated instruction cards, showing borrowers how to operate the most delicate items. In the following quarter, the number of damage reports fell to nine. The library did not claim that the change alone had caused the reduction. Its budget for spare parts remained unchanged, and some larger objects still developed waiting lists because staff needed extra time to inspect them after each return.

**C.** To bring more people into the project, the library began repair workshops on the second Saturday of each month. The sessions were open to residents whether or not they held a library card. Volunteers helped visitors examine small household goods, while reminding them that some problems were unsafe to tackle without a professional. Desk lamps were brought in more often than any other item; broken zips and headphones were also common. The workshops had no minimum age requirement, and staff did not record the ages of visitors. Their main aim was to help people understand why objects stopped working, even if they could not be fixed that day.

**D.** Six months into the pilot, the library collected feedback through a questionnaire completed by 120 participants. Of these, 74 said they had bought fewer new household products since joining the scheme. These were self-reported habits, and the team had not measured any change in carbon emissions. Some respondents said it was inconvenient to cross town to return bulky objects. To address this, librarians proposed testing collection lockers near the train station the following year. The proposal was still under review when the report was written, and no lockers had been installed. The library planned a second survey before deciding whether to expand the programme.

### Q1 — TRUE

- **Evidence / 定位：** Paragraph A: “launched its Object Lending Corner in 2021”
- **解析：** 原文明确说明该项目于2021年启动，题干与原文一致。
- **考点：** `dates`

### Q2 — FALSE

- **Evidence / 定位：** Paragraph A: “Each object could be borrowed for seven days”
- **解析：** 题干是14天，原文是7天，存在明确冲突。
- **考点：** `numbers`

### Q3 — FALSE

- **Evidence / 定位：** Paragraph A: “no borrowing fee was charged”
- **解析：** 原文明确表示免收借用费，与题干相反。
- **考点：** `negation`

- **解析：** 原文只说志愿者协助检查物品，未交代其职业资质；不能推断他们全是电工，也无法证实相反说法。

- **解析：** 原文说明备件预算保持不变，与“increased”直接冲突。

### Q6 — FALSE
