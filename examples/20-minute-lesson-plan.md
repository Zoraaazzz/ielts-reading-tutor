# 20-Minute IELTS Reading Teaching Demo

**Target:** IELTS Reading 4.5–6.0; bilingual Chinese-English support  
**Source:** Original, fictional *Borrow, Learn, Repair: A Library Experiment* (`assets/sample-exercise.json`)  
**Main objective:** Distinguish TRUE, FALSE and NOT GIVEN using exact textual evidence, not general knowledge.

| Time | What the teacher does | What students do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 min | Show Q1. Highlight `began` and `2021`. | Locate “launched ... in 2021” in paragraph A. |
| 3–7 min | Model Q2: `fourteen days` versus `seven days`. Explain contradiction = FALSE. | Explain in one sentence why Q2 is FALSE. |
| 7–12 min | Guide Q4. Ask: does “volunteers helped” prove formal qualifications? | Identify the missing detail and choose NOT GIVEN. |
| 12–17 min | Give Q3, Q5, Q7 without answers. Provide paragraph locations only if needed. | Work independently, underline evidence, compare modifiers. |
| 17–20 min | Review answers. Compare NOT GIVEN and FALSE; teach one self-check. | Write: “A missing detail is NOT GIVEN; explicit opposite evidence is FALSE.” |

## Teacher talk sample / 中英双语示范

“我们先找题目中的限制信息。Q2 says *fourteen days*, and Paragraph A says *seven days*. These numbers contradict each other, so the answer is **FALSE**. 这道题的核心是数字信息发生了直接冲突。”

“Now Q4: *Every volunteer is a qualified electrician.* The text tells us volunteers helped visitors, but it gives no qualifications. 原文既没有证明他们全是电工，也没有证明他们都没有资格，所以答案是 **NOT GIVEN**。”

## Exit ticket

Give one new statement: “The library measured a reduction in carbon emissions.” Have learners locate paragraph D (“had not measured any change in carbon emissions”) and answer **FALSE**, with one Chinese sentence explaining why.

**Note:** This is a small demonstration plan, not an official IELTS lesson or official practice material.
